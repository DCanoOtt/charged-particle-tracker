3. Layers and materials

  • Add layer (dashed “+” tile) appends a layer; click a layer to edit it. Below the stack you can move, duplicate or remove the selected layer. Under each layer the energy it absorbed per primary is shown after a run.
  • Thickness in nm, µm, mm, cm, m or as areal density (mg/cm², g/cm²). A vacuum gap is available in the preset list.
  • Form: solid, liquid or gas. Composition by: atoms (a formula such as H2O, Al2O3, C8H8) or mass fractions (percent or any relative masses). Type a formula in the quick-entry box and press Set composition.
  • Composites: in mass-fraction mode add a “Material” component to mix other presets or your saved materials by mass.
  • Isotopes: natural abundances are used by default. Open Isotopes on an element to change the abundances (e.g. enrich ⁶Li or use deuterium); 100 % selects a single isotope, Reset to natural restores the default.
  • Density: solids and liquids use the value you enter (elements default to their tabulated density). Gases use the ideal-gas law from pressure (kPa, bar, atm, mbar, Pa or Torr), temperature (K) and molar mass; switch it off to give a density directly.
  • Mean excitation energy I is computed from Bragg additivity of the elements (ICRU 37) unless you type a value; compounds in the preset list carry their ICRU values. Use the I-value override for materials where the compound value matters.
  • My materials: Save stores a material in this browser; it can be reused as a layer or as a component of a composite.
