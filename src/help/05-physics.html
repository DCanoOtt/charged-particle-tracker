5. Physics options

  • δ-ray production above the threshold Tcut: energy transfers larger than Tcut are simulated one by one as knock-on electrons (Møller for electrons, the Bethe spectrum for heavy particles); smaller transfers are part of the continuous restricted loss. A lower threshold is more detailed and slower. Transport the δ-electrons follows them through the layers; otherwise their energy is deposited at the production point.
  • Scattering: multiple scattering (Gaussian, Molière-type width) plus single hard Rutherford scattering events. Straggling: Gaussian energy-loss fluctuations of the continuous part with a Lindhard–Scharff correction at low energy. Nuclear stopping: ZBL elastic-recoil energy loss.
  • Nuclear reactions: the projectile is removed at the reaction point (geometric + Coulomb-barrier cross section, scalable with Reaction σ scale). With a proton, deuteron, triton, ³He or α beam and the matching TENDL library loaded, the cross sections come from the library and secondaries are tallied (sections 10 and 11).
  • Step size is the fraction of the remaining range per step (default 0.1; smaller is more accurate and slower). Cut-offs are the energies at which an ion or electron is absorbed on the spot.

Model summary. Electronic stopping from tabulated reference data (selectable under Stopping powers and models): ICRU Report 90 (p, He, C, e⁻ in water/air/graphite), ICRU 73 update (Li–Ar in water/air), NIST PSTAR (protons, d, t by velocity scaling), NIST ASTAR (⁴He, ³He) and ICRU Report 73 (Li … Ar ions, 25 keV/u – 1 GeV/u) for 25 elements and ~50 ICRU/NIST compounds (the compound table is used automatically when the composition matches, otherwise Bragg additivity of the elements); elements missing from a table are interpolated in Z using the semi-empirical model as shape; ions with Z > 18 are scaled from the Ar-ion table at equal v/Z12/3; above the tables Bethe–Bloch matched at the table edge; below them S ∝ v. Electrons: collision stopping from ICRU 90 for water/air/graphite, else the ICRU 37 formula; radiative stopping by formula. Nuclear stopping: ZBL, split into explicitly simulated hard recoils and a continuous soft part. Electronic straggling: Bohr variance with Lindhard–Scharff reduction at low velocity; explicit δ-ray production above the threshold; screened-Rutherford (Wentzel/Molière) scattering with hard single events; removal-type nuclear reactions. Tables reproduce NIST/ICRU values by construction (energy-loss means within ~0.1–0.5 % in tests); expect a few % for the interpolated elements and larger uncertainty for Z > 18 ions, electrons and nuclear reactions. Tables: NIST PSTAR/ASTAR, ICRU 73 and 90, accessed through the libdedx project (APTG).
