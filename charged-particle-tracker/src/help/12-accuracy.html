12. Accuracy and limits

  • Expected accuracy is about 1–3 % in the Bethe region, 5–10 % at low energies in light materials and ±20–30 % for heavy ions in heavy targets below ~1 MeV/u. Cross-check important numbers against SRIM, PSTAR/ASTAR/ESTAR or Geant4.
  • Photons (bremsstrahlung) and secondaries from nuclear reactions are not transported; their energy is reported in the energy balance. For the five projectiles with a library the reaction yields and the secondary spectra are tallied from the library (sections 10-11): they are no better than the evaluation (TENDL is model-based), the Kalbach slope is an estimate when the file gives only r, and in two-body mode the lab spectra assume that the tabulated E′ is the CM energy.
  • Layers are laterally infinite. Very fast heavy ions with a low δ-ray threshold can take a long time; raise Tcut or lower the number of primaries first.
