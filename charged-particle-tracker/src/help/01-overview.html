1. What the program does

It follows charged particles (electrons, protons, deuterons, tritons, ³He, α and heavier ions) through a stack of layers by Monte Carlo. Each primary particle loses energy continuously (electronic and nuclear stopping with straggling), is deflected by Coulomb scattering, may knock out δ-electrons that are tracked themselves, and may undergo a nuclear reaction (simple model). Everything is tallied per layer, per depth and per particle. Units are metric; lengths in the tallies are internally cm and energies MeV.

Quick start: (1) click the layer to choose its material and thickness, (2) set the beam, (3) press Run simulation, (4) read the summary and open the plot tabs. Any change needs a new run; the stopping-power plot is the only one that updates without running.
