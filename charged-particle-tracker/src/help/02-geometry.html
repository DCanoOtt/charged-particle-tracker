2. Geometry and conventions

  • Layers are infinite slabs perpendicular to the z axis, stacked in the order shown (left = first). The beam enters the first layer at z = 0. Particles that leave through the front are backscattered, through the back transmitted.
  • The default beam direction is (0, 0, 1). The direction vector (dx, dy, dz) is normalised; dz must be positive. The tilt field sets an angle θ from z in the x–z plane. Tilting increases the path length in every layer.
  • Depth z is always the z coordinate of the particle, not the path length. x and y are the lateral coordinates.
