11.3 Reaction probability and yields. With σi(E) the library cross section of isotope i (barn) and ni its atom density (abundance × number density of the material):

P = Σi ni · 10−24 · Σk σi(Ek) Lk, dP/dE = ni 10−24 σi(Ek) Lk / ΔE

The yield of a particle x is the same sum weighted with its multiplicity yx(E) (the MF 6 product yield of the file; for channels without MF 6 products the multiplicities follow from the reaction, e.g. 2 neutrons for (α,2n)). Residual nuclei: Z,A of target + projectile − emitted particles. “All channels” adds every reaction channel of the file except the lumped sums: MT 3 (non-elastic), and MT 4 and 103–107 whenever their components (MT 51–91, 600–849) are present in the file. In a TENDL alpha file such as a-Ar040.tendl the components are absent, so MT 4, 5, 16, 17, 22, 24, 28, 29, 32–34, 41, 44, 45, 102–108 and 111–117 are all added and their sum reproduces MT 3 (within 0.1 % over the whole energy grid of that file; MT 4 there is only (α,n), and MT 5 is used only above 30 MeV). Production cross sections such as MT 201–207 are never added. MT 4 and 103–107 are replaced by their level channels only when those add up to them (within 3 %); otherwise the sum is used and the levels are dropped. If the total still differs from MT 3 by more than 3 % the page warns, and the expandable “Check of the channel sum, per isotope” table under the reaction table shows, for each isotope, the flux-weighted ratio, the energy of the largest discrepancy and the channels added. Below the plots there are two collapsible tables: the first for the channels ticked in the menu, the second (“Everything produced”) always for all channels, independently of the ticked ones. Both list reactions, neutrons, protons, deuterons, tritons, He-3, α, γ and “rest” (residual nuclei), all per primary. The consistency line compares Σ channels, MT 3 and the number of reactions counted in the transport. Photon yields and spectra exist only where MF 6 lists them (TENDL gives discrete lines plus a continuum, also for (α,γ)); photons are taken as isotropic without Doppler shift, so their plot shows one per-sr curve and the integrated spectrum. Recoil nuclei use the residual-nucleus entries of MF 6 (CM energy distribution, isotropic in the CM) and the same two-body boost; heavy recoils only go forward, so the large-angle curves are empty. The elastic channel (MT 2: scattered α and recoil, charged-particle elastic law) is not a reaction and is not shown. Secondaries are tallied, not transported.
