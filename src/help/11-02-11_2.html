11.2 Ion flux. The path length ℓ of each step is distributed over the energy interval [E1, E0] swept in that step: every energy bin receives ℓ times the fraction of the interval it overlaps (exact; no dependence on the step size). The result Lk is the total path length per primary in energy bin k of width ΔE (cm). The Ion flux plot shows Φ(E)/Φ₀ = L/(layer thickness) per energy unit; at the exit of a layer the plane-crossing flux is tallied instead.
