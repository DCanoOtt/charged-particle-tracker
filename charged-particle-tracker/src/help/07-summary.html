7. Results summary

Fractions of primaries that stopped in the target, were transmitted, backscattered or reacted; the projected range ⟨z⟩ with its straggling σ; mean energy deposit; δ-rays per primary. The Layers table gives, per primary, the energy deposited in each layer (total, ion electronic, ion nuclear, δ-electrons), the energy lost by the primary itself, δ-rays and reactions. The Energy balance table accounts for the whole beam energy: deposits, escaping primaries and secondaries, bremsstrahlung (photons are not tracked) and projectile energy removed by reactions; the residual should be ≈ 0.
